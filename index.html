LeaseForce

A leasing platform for landlords, agencies and anyone with space to lease. You set up your properties, units and tax settings once. After that, the only regular work is adding a tenant and a lease, or closing one. LeaseForce handles billing, reminders, payment reconciliation and owner reports on its own.

I built it alone in six part-time months, directing AI coding agents as my engineering team: I set the architecture, broke the work into phases with verifiable outcomes, and checked every result. Each customer's data is isolated by the database itself, an AI assistant answers portfolio questions and acts only after the user confirms, and production runs on self-managed servers with a standby replica and zero-downtime deploys.

U2Story

An electronics engineer who kept following the systems underneath.

I studied Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the University of Nairobi, and spent my spare time at a makerspace designing parts in CAD and 3D printing them. I liked building things from scratch, and I still do.

At Safaricom I sat on the Change Advisory Board, where every high-risk change to the country's largest telco network was argued over before it went live. That taught me the habit I bring to every migration: plan for the failure before you plan for the success.

After an international machine-learning internship and a published SVM diagnostic model, I joined China Mobile International. Over six years I moved from solutions consultant to senior cloud and infrastructure lead. I mirror each customer's environment in a lab and break it there first, which is how a 1,000 VM bank migration ends with no disruptive downtime.

Outside work I built LeaseForce, a leasing platform now live with four pilot customers. I architected and shipped it, directing AI coding agents as my engineering team: I owned the multi-tenant design, the phased plan, the specs, the load-test fixes, and when to reset an agent that had lost the thread. Directing agents to ship a real system is the skill I want to bring to my next role.
